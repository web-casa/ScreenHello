## From a screenshot to a finished visual

Import a local image, paste from the clipboard or try the bundled example. Add space, a background, a browser frame and annotations for a product update, tutorial or social post. The editor processes your images on your device; no account or cloud sync is required.

### Paragraphs and backgrounds

Add padding, rounded corners, a shadow and an inner border. Choose a solid colour, a gradient, one of the bundled image backgrounds, or a local image of your own.

### Frames and annotations

Wrap the screenshot in browser chrome or a raster device frame. Arrows, step numbers and magnifiers point at the part that matters, and the exported canvas keeps the result.

> Everything runs in your browser. There is no account, no upload and no cloud sync, so a screenshot never leaves your device.

## Edit once. Keep the project.

Arrange up to 12 image layers, undo changes and save a portable .screenhello project. A project file is your own backup; an automatically saved browser draft is a separate local copy and may be cleared by the browser.


## A Web tool with clear boundaries

Export PNG, JPG, WebP or AVIF and compare a compression preview before downloading. The Web editor is the available workflow described here. Desktop builds are still test candidates, and a Chrome/Edge extension is planned, not offered as a released download. AVIF offers standard export only, without quality controls or compression preview.
